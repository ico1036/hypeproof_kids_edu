# HYROX license/certification model research

작성: 2026-05-15

## Sources checked

- HYROX World Championships page: https://hyrox.com/hyrox-world-championships/
- HYROX Athlete License page: https://hyrox.com/athlete-license/
- HYROX Training Clubs page: https://hyrox.com/find-a-hyrox-partner-gym/
- HYROX Coaches directory: https://coaches.hyrox.com/
- HYROX365 Academy coaches education article: https://www.hyrox365.com/about-hyrox/hyrox365-insights/advances-into-coaches-education

## Findings

### 1. Participation is open; license is not required for ordinary racing

HYROX is open-entry for normal races. Search snippets and rulebook snippets indicate no qualification is required for ordinary Singles/Doubles participation. This is important: HYROX does not make the entry product gated by certification.

### 2. World Championship qualification is performance-based

World Championships qualify by achieving top placings in age group/division at global races. Qualification is not “course completion”; it is race performance. Pro Division is generally required for qualification, with exceptions noted by HYROX.

### 3. Athlete License is separate from participation

HYROX Athlete License:
- Not required for general participation.
- Intended for athletes aspiring to qualify for Elite 15 / top race series.
- Required for Elite 15 athletes.
- From 2025-10-23, license required for race times to be eligible for qualification/ranking points.
- Requires anti-doping compliance/testing commitment.
- Valid one season.
- Costs EUR 197 + VAT.
- Includes eligibility review.

Interpretation: The athlete license is a paid governance/ranking/eligibility layer, not a class-completion certificate.

### 4. Coach certification and coach affiliation are distinct

HYROX365 Academy offers courses:
- Foundation Course: Race Ready
- Level 1 Course: Creating Athletes

Both are required for HYROX Performance Coaches. But HYROX365 explicitly says: completion of Academy courses does not qualify anyone as a Performance Coach. Only if affiliated, a coach becomes a HYROX Performance Coach.

Interpretation: HYROX separates education completion from official commercial permission to offer HYROX training.

### 5. Training Club accreditation is facility license / brand usage

HYROX Training Clubs are accredited affiliated facilities. They can advertise and market services while leveraging the HYROX brand name. Official clubs get access to HYROX Performance Hub: daily programming tools, class tutorials, marketing materials, etc.

Interpretation: The gym/partner license is brand + content + programming + marketing access, not merely a certificate.

## Implication for HypeProof

Do not copy “4 weeks complete = license.” That is too weak and not HYROX-like.

Better mapping:

- Open participation: anyone can join HypeProof Kids Game Lab / Challenge.
- Result record: participant gets a score/result/report from a standardized challenge.
- Certificate/badge: completion/achievement artifact, not license.
- Athlete-style license: optional paid season membership for ranking eligibility, competition qualification, portfolio profile, renewal.
- Coach certification: course completion + assessment.
- Facilitator affiliation: only after affiliation/approval can one commercially run HypeProof-certified classes.
- Partner center license: facility/institution gets brand usage + curriculum + facilitator dashboard + marketing kit.

Key distinction:
- Certificate = I completed / achieved.
- License = I am allowed to participate in ranked ecosystem or commercially use the brand/IP.
- Accreditation = my institution is allowed to host/market official HypeProof programs.
